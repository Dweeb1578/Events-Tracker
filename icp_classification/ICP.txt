Refined ICP as of 3rd March 2025
Overview - We need to target $50k+ ACV deals to have a higher right to win and to be a profitable motion.
For $40k+ deals (companies that have $10M+ revenue) our sweet spot is:
Our right to win is highest when a company has: Complex pricing + looking for billing automation + rev-rec automation
Inhouse tools, legacy tools: Stripe/Chargebee/Netsuite don't compete in the category as they don't solve this use case. Our main competition here becomes Maxio, Zuora and Ordway only vs 10+ products
Rev-rec becomes important only when there is at least 1 senior finance person in the team, otherwise the company perhaps doesn't care about rev-rec
ICP - Ideal Customer Profile
Employee size: 150-2000 (Maxio and Ordaway operate here)
Companies based (HQ) out of US, UK, India, Aus
What do we not cater:
Pure Services

Min 1 senior Finance person is in the team: CFO, Controller, VP/Director/Head/Lead/Senior Manager of Finance or Accounting or FP&A or Ops and Finance or COO
Controller is the head of accounting and their team does rev-rec/rev-accounting
Nuanced personas in larger companies: deal desk, receivables, accountant, CPA
Our key buyers are CFO minus 1 = Controller, VP/Director/Head/Lead/Senior Manager of Finance or Accounting or FP&A
Not all of our target companies might have a CFO because CFOs are hired when the company is in the $10-$50M revenue range and this is a wide range.
Controllers are hired when the company is between $5-$10M in revenue and is acting CFO until the CFO is hired
P1 personas:
CFO
Bizops, Revops Biz Systems etc. -> people who handle tech stack and data integration
Additional Points -
Engineers: while they are not buyers and wouldn't necessarily come on first demos, they become decision makers later in the process when the opportunity progresses to advanced stages. We need to influence them through the Inbound content (webinar, blog, ads etc) and the @pipeteam (multi thread, share value added content, warm connections).
They can become blockers to the migration process and we need to actively influence them. Many times they don't even come on demos and can yet act as blockers.
Opportunistically (10% bandwidth), we will also be targeting companies with 1k-10k employees since our product can solve for such complexity and getting 1 such customer ($100k+ ACV) will open the way to get many more.
